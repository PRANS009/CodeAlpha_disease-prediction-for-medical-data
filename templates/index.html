10-Step End-to-End Machine Learning Workflow

Complete lifecycle from raw clinical patient records to scaled classification and inference:

01

Patient Data Collection

Ingested 303 patient records with 14 clinical features from the UCI Machine Learning Cleveland Heart Disease repository.

02

Data Cleaning & Imputation

Inspected null values ('?'). Imputed missing discrete fluoroscopy (ca: 4 records) and thallium (thal: 2 records) with modes. Verified 0 duplicate rows.

03

Feature & Target Separation

Extracted 13 clinical predictors ($X$) and binarized heart disease target ($y$): class 0 for Absent (<50% narrowing) and classes 1-4 mapped to 1 for Present.

04

Stratified Train / Test Split

Partitioned data into 80% training set (242 samples) and 20% test set (61 unseen samples) preserving the 54.1% / 45.9% class ratio.

05

Feature Scaling

Applied StandardScaler fit strictly on training set and transformed both training and unseen testing sets to avoid data leakage.

06

Classification Model Training

Trained 4 distinct algorithms: Logistic Regression, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest, and XGBoost.

07

Unseen Data Testing

Evaluated generalization ability on strictly unseen held-out test records to ensure no overfitting.

08

Multi-Metric Evaluation

Computed Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, ROC-AUC score, and complete 2x2 Confusion Matrices.

09

Model Comparison & Benchmarking

Compared all classifiers side-by-side. Random Forest achieved the highest F1 Score (88.14%) and ROC-AUC (95.89%).

10

New Patient Prediction

Deployed saved estimators and scaler for real-time single patient prediction, probability output, and clinical risk analysis.

Clinical Feature Dictionary & Medical Interpretation

age

Patient age in completed years. Heart disease risk steadily rises with increasing age.

sex

Biological sex (1 = male, 0 = female). Statistically, males show higher premature coronary artery incidence.

cp (Chest Pain Type)

1: Typical angina, 2: Atypical angina, 3: Non-anginal pain, 4: Asymptomatic. In ischemic heart disease datasets, asymptomatic cases paradoxically correlate strongly with late-stage diagnosis.

trestbps (Resting Blood Pressure)

Resting arterial pressure in mm Hg upon hospital admission. Values > 130 indicate hypertension.

chol (Serum Cholesterol)

Total serum cholesterol in mg/dl. Desirable levels are < 200 mg/dl; ≥ 240 mg/dl signifies elevated hypercholesterolemia.

fbs (Fasting Blood Sugar)

Fasting blood sugar > 120 mg/dl (1 = true, 0 = false). Indicator of insulin resistance or underlying diabetes mellitus.

restecg (Resting ECG)

0: Normal, 1: ST-T wave abnormality (T wave inversions or ST displacement > 0.05 mV), 2: Left ventricular hypertrophy.

thalach (Max Heart Rate Achieved)

Peak heart rate (beats per minute) during treadmill stress testing. Inability to reach expected target HR indicates reduced cardiac reserve.

exang (Exercise-Induced Angina)

Development of angina symptoms during exercise stress test (1 = yes, 0 = no). Strong indicator of myocardial ischemia.

oldpeak (ST Depression)

ST segment depression induced by exercise relative to rest. Reflects the degree of subendocardial ischemia.

slope (ST Segment Slope)

Slope of peak exercise ST segment: 1 = Upsloping (mild/favorable), 2 = Flat (ischemia sign), 3 = Downsloping (severe ischemia).

ca (Major Fluoroscopy Vessels)

Number of major coronary blood vessels (0 to 3) showing narrowing when examined under fluoroscopy.

thal (Thallium Heart Scintigraphy)

3 = Normal perfusion, 6 = Fixed defect (prior myocardial necrosis), 7 = Reversible defect (active inducible ischemia).
